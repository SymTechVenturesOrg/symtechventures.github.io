## Why not regenerate all 28 paper illustrations?

The [actual 28-file scientific and caption review](reports/V2_STAGE_09_SYMMETRIA_28_FIGURE_SCIENTIFIC_EDITORIAL_REVIEW_20261117.md) identified **three priority claim-bearing graphics** in need of substantial correction/qualification, **multiple misplaced or mismatched captions/figure placements**, and many other images usable as conceptual illustrations. A filename/section mismatch does **not** imply that the image itself is bad.

**First corrections, not a 28-image compulsory redraw:**
- Paper 1 `01_symmetria_perspective.webp`: `N²=0` alone does not entail `NA+AN=0` for arbitrary A.
- Paper 2 `02_noether_symmetry_conservation.webp`: universal mirror/parity and time-reversal invariance statements are inaccurate; Noether's continuous versus discrete symmetry distinction needs care.
- Paper 3 `03_dirac_nilpotent_structure.webp`: acknowledge Rowlands' *specified* nilpotent Dirac algebraic correspondence rather than imply generic `N²=0` proves equivalence or a new physics result.
- Paper 3's image subjects suggest **two pairs of source placements** may need swapping (Figures 1.1/3.1 and 5.1/6.1); other Papers 2/4 captions need reconciliation. See the [minimal reference-free editorial plan](reports/V2_STAGE_09_ROWLANDS_DISCUSSION_PAPER_MINIMAL_EDITORIAL_PLAN_20261117.md).
- Remaining illustrations can stay until a later optional cosmetic decision; scientific clarity matters more than unnecessary re-generation.

## Phase 2 — New website-level imagery, only after layout freeze

**Scope:** Imagery for the revised website's actual image slots, starting with the **homepage hero decision** and whichever section/programme artwork Phase 1 approved. Preserve all 46 old raster assets for rollback. There is **no automatic requirement to redo six logos or twelve historical icons/banners**. Old historical images remain on noindex pages unless a concrete design need is agreed.

- Make a **small pilot** (ideally 1–2 compositions) to lock palette, crop/format and visual consistency. All outputs should be new AI-generated conceptual artwork, **not presented as actual NASA or mission photographs**.
- Choose exact final assets by page/slot and owner approval before committing. Proposed Earthrise may be supplied/approved; do not pretend the absent previously discussed 716×494 asset is in GitHub.
- Convert **WebP at quality 80 (encoder quality value 80)**; it does **not** mean 80% smaller. Use measured file sizes, legibility and visually acceptable crop quality. For small logos or text/detail-heavy assets, compare lossless / higher quality if necessary.
- Asset filenames, provenance notes, size/responsiveness `<picture>`/`srcset` as appropriate, alt text, no broken links; use Codex Luna Medium for deterministic conversion/integration, browser and performance checks.
- No embedded mathematical expressions unless separately designed/verified; prefer source-rendered text/equations over AI-text inside images.
- **Acceptance:** every approved website-level image placed and tested on actual 390/1440 px renders; no false photography claim; quality/size and performance checked; owner accepts. Only then start Phase 3.

**Indicative effort:** ~**4–10 active hours**, highly dependent on how many actual approved image slots exist and how often visual regeneration is needed. Do not invent the number of required new images before Phase 1.

## Phase 3 — Selective SYMMETRIA discussion-paper illustration corrections

**Scope:** Correct scientific overstatements and mismatched placement/captions in the four **reference-free** Rowlands-inspired discussion papers. Preserve all 28 original WebPs for provenance/rollback. **Do not automatically regenerate all 28.**

- **3A — Accuracy:** prioritise the three high-value graphics in Papers 1, 2 and 3, with concise scientifically sound graphic wording; maintain the established dark design and understandable discussion tone. Do not claim the wider theoretical programme has been validated.
- **3B — Placement and captions:** reconcile Paper 3's two possible figure swaps and review Paper 2/4 mismatches; reuse suitable existing images where the issue is only wrong placement or inaccurate caption.
- **3C — Optional cosmetic consistency:** only after assessing retained figures against the new site art. Regenerate another figure **only if specific visual or factual defect warrants it**; document the owner's decision.
- **WebP quality 80**, with visually inspected text/equation readability. For precise labels/equations, use verified vector/HTML overlays or a controlled composition pipeline rather than relying on generative text accuracy.
- **Codex:** handle narrow page image integration, filenames, alt/caption changes and rerun full regression after the owner-approved paper-main integrity baseline is deliberately revised. Chat leads visual generation/editorial correctness; **Terra only for documented genuinely challenging issues with owner approval**.
- **Acceptance:** actual corrected graphics, owner-reviewed scientific caveats, aligned figure/caption map, seven illustrations per original paper, all four preserved URLs, no academic bibliography, regression/accessibility pass, and rollback record.

**Indicative effort:** ~**5–12 active hours** for the three priority redraws plus mapping/QA; additional redraws add time. Number of final image-generation iterations is not predictable upfront.
