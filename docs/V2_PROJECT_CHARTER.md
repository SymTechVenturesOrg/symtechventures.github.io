## Mission and audience

SymTech Ventures v2.0 is the public home of an **independent research project focused primarily on space exploration**, intended for curious readers, learners and technically interested people. Its purpose is to share material clearly, not to pitch a Silicon Valley start-up, recruit founding fellows, suggest funding has been secured, or imply a mature research institution.

We distinguish established physics, existing literature, speculative SYMMETRIA hypotheses, verified mathematical calculations, computer simulations and empirical observations. We must not suggest unvalidated propulsion or space technology is operational.

## Defined v2.0 release scope

- New accessible, responsive visual identity, homepage, navigation, common page shell and footer.
- Space-focused research overview, with SYMMETRIA, NSRP and SEIP explained separately.
- **SYMMETRIA:** preserve its existing 4×4 discussion series, with the complete readable content, illustrations and public URLs of **Papers 1–4**.
- **BridgePoint:** 4 categories × 4 planned mathematics/physics lessons (**16 titles**), plus **one written and reviewed pilot lesson**.
- **NSRP:** five original research stage projects × four phases (**20 phases**) as an evidence-labelled work plan; no fictitious results.
- **SEIP:** five engineering companion stage projects × four phases (**20 phases**), describing reproducible modelling questions and constraints rather than validated devices.
- **Discussion Papers** index for the existing accessible essays and clearly marked future entries.
- **Publications:** an intentionally **empty formal publication catalogue**, with a plain empty-state explanation.
- About and Contact sections, including the user-supplied LinkedIn profile https://www.linkedin.com/in/drgeoffthomas.
- Reuse suitable original scientific images selectively after quality, accessibility and provenance checks.
- Keep existing domain, GitHub Pages host, correct SEO metadata and sitemap, and workable legacy page paths.

## Explicit exclusions

Do **not** copy the entire old website design, archive, Drive repositories or Aletheia Core; do not write all sixteen full BridgePoint lessons during first launch; do not invent completed NSRP/SEIP studies or create a commercial sales/funding website.

## GitHub and publication governance

- The live `main` branch remains untouched until a **separate, explicit release approval**.
- This charter, the [development plan](WEBSITE_V2_WORKPLAN.md), [operating workflow](WEBSITE_DEVELOPMENT_WORKFLOW.md), [site map](SPACE_FIRST_SITE_PLAN.md) and [programme structures](PROGRAMME_SERIES_STRUCTURE.md) live in the documentation-only planning PR.
- Codex creates its own **implementation branch and PR** for HTML/CSS/JS. Neither branch nor draft PR counts as a production-ready release.
- Record selected images, original article URLs, citation/provenance concerns, outstanding checks and rollback instructions.
- Work briefs must have exact inputs, named deliverables, acceptance criteria, exclusions and a short **COPY TO CHAT** handback.
- Source-level scientific facts stay traceable; a website claim must be corrected or qualified if the evidence changes.
- Use Dr T Style Updates at current Europe/London time: `[HH:MM:SS] NN% — factual status; ETA ~time`. A percentage refers to the **whole named task**, not elapsed time or token usage. Avoid claiming 100% before verification.
