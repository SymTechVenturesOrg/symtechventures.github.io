## Figure-specific visual inspection

### Figure 2.1 — Natural Symmetry Patterns

The sunflower is no longer overlaid with an alleged exact Fibonacci/golden-rectangle fit. The remaining solar flower-head geometry, shell growth form, sixfold ice crystal, tree branching and water ripples illustrate different recurring patterns without claiming one exact mathematical mechanism underlies them all. The footer explicitly distinguishes biological from physical reasons. Midnight-navy scene, luminous cyan and gold lighting, elegant gold-serif heading and caption panels remain consistent with the approved cinematic series. Text in the smaller callouts will not be reliably legible at narrow mobile widths without zoom/HTML description. **No additional image correction recommended.**

### Figure 3.1 — Mathematical Symmetry and Invariance

The central **idealised sixfold** snowflake, rotation and reflection panels support a legitimate `D₆` rotational/reflection symmetry example. The translation box explicitly explains that translation **moves the finite shape to a different position**, with congruence rather than invariance in place, and the visible coordinate mapping `x′ = x + a, y′ = y + b` is consistent with that choice. There is no old misleading claim that a displaced finite snowflake is unchanged in the original position. The gold/cyan dimensional diagrams and dark-space typography match the visual family, although densely mathematical small panels require accessible selectable explanations and full-size links on the eventual website. **No further correction recommended.**

### Figure 6.1 — Could Deeper Symmetry Shape Physical Law?

The new single continuous geometrical illustration replaces the previous unrelated motif/planetary-atom collage. Subject title is an interrogative science question, not a project masthead; labels describe a **hypothetical** sequence: hidden symmetry → constraints → emergent structure → observable patterns. A conspicuous visible footer clearly reads **“Exploratory hypothesis — not established physics”**. That qualifier prevents the conceptual causal arrows from reasonably presenting an experimentally validated SYMMETRIA mechanism; any future accessible HTML description must repeat this status. The artwork matches the cover's restrained cinematic spatial depth and colour language. **No further correction recommended.**
