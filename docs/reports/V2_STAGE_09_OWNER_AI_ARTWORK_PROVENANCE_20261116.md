## Owner's original statement

In response to the question, “Do you know how the original SYMMETRIA paper illustrations and logos were created? Were they principally AI-generated under your direction, created personally, commissioned, or obtained from elsewhere?”, the project owner replied:

> They were generated by ChatGPT (aka Synthman). 😉

## What this establishes

- The project owner **attests** that the **original SYMMETRIA paper illustrations and logos** were generated through ChatGPT under their direction.
- The earlier “creation source unknown” label can therefore be refined to **“ChatGPT-generated — creation method confirmed by project owner (16 November 2026)”** for the original group described in the question: **28 paper-specific illustrations and six common logos** in the initial Stage 9 register. This is a group-level owner statement; file-by-file ChatGPT generation sessions, prompts and downloadable originals were not independently examined.
- The Stage 8C register finds **46 distinct referenced raster assets**, comprising **28 paper illustrations, six shared logos, and 12 additional historical banners/icons/hero assets**. The owner's answer was in response to the paper illustrations and logos, **not an explicit file-by-file assertion covering all 12 other historical graphics**. Their origins should not silently be assumed.
- This provenance statement does **not** itself substantiate third-party rights clearance, originality, commercial-use permissions under applicable terms, absence of protected elements, or ownership of any external materials incorporated into generated output. This document makes no legal conclusion or new licence grant.
- It does **not** verify equations, captions, physics claims, parity/T-reversal assertions or nilpotent/Dirac equivalence in the illustrations. Mathematical/educational content remains a separate editorial review.
- The **proposed Earthrise homepage WebP** was not present in the Codex Stage 8C checkout and is **not covered** by this attestation unless the actual file is located and its source confirmed. The homepage still uses the prior inline orbit illustration.
