Nilpotent Symmetry Research Programme (NSRP)

NSRP is a proposed mathematical and theoretical research programme examining nilpotent structures and possible SYMMETRIA extensions. It asks whether a formulation is consistent, how it relates to known mathematics, and what could count against a physical interpretation.

Planned programme: Project phases describe proposed work, not completed validation. NCP phase labels are provisional and follow a historical companion crosswalk; detailed descriptions require reconciliation with an authoritative NCP plan.
