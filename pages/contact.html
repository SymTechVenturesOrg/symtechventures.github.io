Independent research / Contact

Contact SymTech Ventures

SymTech Ventures is an independent research project exploring mathematics, theoretical physics and their possible relevance to space exploration. This page explains the current contact arrangements and directs readers to the research already available online.

Current contact arrangements

Online enquiries are temporarily paused. The previous enquiry form has been suspended while privacy, third-party processing and anti-spam safeguards are reviewed. No new submissions are accepted through this website.

Enquiry status
