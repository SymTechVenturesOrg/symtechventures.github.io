Independent research / Space exploration

SymTech Engineering & Innovation Programme (SEIP)

SEIP is the engineering and computational companion to NSRP. It asks what can be modelled and assessed with clear assumptions, reproducible calculations and established physical constraints.

Planned programme: These phases are plans, not functioning engineering projects, completed simulations, verified spacecraft technology or evidence of a new physical effect. A simulation is not a demonstrated physical result.

EFP — Engineering Foundations Project

Companion: NSRP NCP.

Planned

1 — Engineering Interpretation

Define what, if anything, can be modelled from established/provisional inputs

Planned

2 — Structural Modelling

Build well-defined, dimensionally consistent reference models

Planned

3 — Engineering Extensions

Explore bounded extensions and clearly state assumptions

Planned

4 — Interpretation and Constraints

Evaluate limits, conservation laws and uncertainty

Planned
