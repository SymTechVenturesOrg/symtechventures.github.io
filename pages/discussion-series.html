SYMMETRIA Discussion Series

This series introduces ideas and open questions connected with SYMMETRIA. Four discussion papers are currently available online; twelve further entries are planned. These essays are shared for reading and discussion. They should not be mistaken for peer-reviewed or experimentally validated research.
