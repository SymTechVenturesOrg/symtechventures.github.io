Independent research / Space exploration

About SymTech Ventures

SymTech Ventures is an independently developed research project centred on questions in mathematical and theoretical physics, with a primary interest in space exploration. It provides a public place to share exploratory work and learning resources as they become ready.

Why ask these questions?

There is value in asking ambitious questions, provided we are clear about the difference between a question and a result. This website documents what is being explored, how ideas are examined and where the limits of current evidence lie.

Research scope

The project brings together SYMMETRIA theoretical questions, the NSRP mathematical research programme, the SEIP computational and engineering research plan, and BridgePoint educational resources. These are proposals or developing activities, not proof of institutional laboratories or mature technologies.

Evidence matters: a mathematical proposal or computer model is not evidence of a new physical effect. Established science, exploratory hypotheses, planned work, simulations and measurements are distinguished throughout the site.
